in `src/circuits/shanghai/zones.json` are available under the Open Database License
(ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ .
Attribution and contributor information: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright .
Original way IDs and retrieval date are retained in each zone's source metadata.

Venue opening status is factual information from Shanghai government's 2026 event
and ticket announcements. Slope/height parameters and C-area boundaries are
documented estimates; they are not OSM survey elevations. No official map artwork
or photographs are distributed in the website.
