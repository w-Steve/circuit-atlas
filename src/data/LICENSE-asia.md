# Asia and Oceania spectator footprint data

© OpenStreetMap contributors. Raw footprint extracts in src/circuits/asia/osm
and OSM-derived boundaries in src/circuits/generated are available under ODbL:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Attribution: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Original OSM way IDs, tags and source dates are retained.
Supplemental Suzuka S boundaries in suzuka-supplemental-zones.json are also
OSM-derived under ODbL. The conflicting attraction tag is resolved using the
dated 2026 venue map and organizer seating/location references, not by deleting
source evidence. Source artwork and photographs remain local research only.

Permanent maximum seating-row counts are factual observations of the organizer
seating charts. Those live charts are undated and currently linked from 2027
pages; they are not used as proof of 2026 ticket sales. The estimated 0.4m riser,
2m structural connection and B2 front safety-rail dimensions are original model
assumptions explicitly separated from the published row counts.

Temporary grandstand footprints are approximate factual interpretations of
publicly available venue diagrams. Sources, diagram years, control pairs and
model assumptions are retained in src/circuits/asia/review.json and
scripts/build-asia-spectators.py. Diagram-derived coordinates and estimated
viewing regions are not official ticket boundaries or surveyed seat positions.
Source map artwork, venue photographs, logos and ticket assets are not included.

Suzuka's organizer-authored 2026 wayfinding map was consulted through a retained
document copy at https://www.coloma.it/images/F1E-Japan-2026-Circuit-Map.pdf .
Melbourne's 2026 diagram is an independent annotated seating guide; it is not
misrepresented as an organizer engineering plan.
Baku position references are from the 2025 organizer wayfinding map, with 2026
name checks recorded separately. Singapore's formerly 2026 URL now serves a
2027 diagram: it is used only as a low-confidence positional reference for
corroborated 2026 named stands, never as evidence of 2026 ticket availability.

All stand heights, slopes, rows, seating-band cuts and fences are estimates.
No circuit or Formula One brand affiliation or seat-view guarantee is implied.

Lusail Hill ordinary General Admission is confirmed by the organizer 2026 FAQ,
event page, August30 news release, and the event map at
https://www.lcsc.qa/sites/default/files/2026-08/eventmap.png .
Only interpreted factual coordinates and source metadata are retained in
asia/lusail-general-admission.json. The original schematic image and organizer
Hill photograph are not distributed. Regional GEDTM30 does not resolve the
constructed grass terraces and retaining walls shown in that photograph.
The candidate remains unavailable after three-device photo acceptance failed;
its approximate registration is not advertised as a usable seat-view survey.
Ordinary Hill admission and the separately sold Hill Lounge are distinct.

Suzuka permanent G1/G2 seating interpretation (2026-10-25) uses the organizer
structural chart https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/eng/f1/ticket/images/seat/pdf/f1_g.pdf
and seat-view aerial https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/eng/f1/ticket/images/seat/g/img_seat_g.jpg .
Only factual row counts, OSM-derived outer envelopes and provenance are retained
in asia/suzuka-g-permanent.json. Original chart, aerial, map artwork and logos
are not distributed. OSM strips remain attributed above; filled aisle/end-taper
areas and vertical profiles are approximate. Current2027 structural references
are not claimed to certify complete2026 public inventory or temporary G3/G4/G5.

Suzuka West Area ground facts (2026-10-25): the dated promoter announcement at
https://www.honda-ml.co.jp/newsrelease/suzuka/2025/08/0828_f1.pdf supports2026
west-ticket families and separate temporary reserved stands. Approximate ground
ribbons are interpreted from the undated organizer detailed image at
https://www.suzukacircuit.jp/eng/f1/ticket/images/seat/west_seat/img_west_map.png .
Its live parent page is now2027, so precise2026 access boundaries are not
certified. Only factual interpreted coordinates, source hashes and uncertainty
are distributed in asia/suzuka-west-ground.json. Original publication, detailed
map, photographs and logos are not bundled. Blue seats, yellow photographer-only
areas, later-season G/H/I admission and unmarked ground are not public grass.

The G permanent earth-bank profile now samples its original front/rear OSM
edges against processed GSI regional elevation (see LICENSE-gsi.md). Original
outer coordinates and numbered-row counts remain. The 0.1m slab clearance and
uniform interpolation between rows are schematic estimates, not measured
individual stair risers or certified stand platforms. The former fixed-rise
configuration remains in the provenance record; no organizer artwork is bundled.

Melbourne Park Pass: factual viewing-mound outline coordinates were interpreted
from the official2026 visitor map recovered from its2026-03-04 web archive.
Only derived coordinate facts and provenance are included, not the PDF, artwork,
logos or branded graphics. Map registration and mound elevation remain approximate;
five modeled locations do not imply complete Park Pass access or a surveyed hill.

canopy completeness are estimates; the former Waite facts remain in provenance.

control comes from the authored turn-marker center projected onto the source
