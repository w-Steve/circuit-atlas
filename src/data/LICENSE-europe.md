Attribution: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright .
Original OSM way IDs, sources and retrieval dates are retained per zone.

Manually interpreted temporary viewing footprints are factual, approximate
placements from 2026 organizer diagrams and public venue descriptions. Source
URLs, ground controls, rectangle parameters and estimated registration offsets
are retained in review.json and scripts/build-europe-spectators.py. Heights,
slopes and fences are estimates, not OSM elevation surveys or seat guarantees.
No organizer map artwork, photographs, logos or ticket assets are distributed.
