# Americas spectator footprint data

https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Attribution: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Original OSM way IDs, tags and source dates are retained.

Temporary grandstand footprints are approximate factual interpretations of
publicly available venue diagrams. Sources, diagram years, control pairs and
model assumptions are retained in src/circuits/americas/review.json and
scripts/build-americas-spectators.py. Diagram-derived coordinates and estimated
viewing regions are not official ticket boundaries or surveyed seat positions.
Source map artwork, venue photographs, logos and ticket assets are not included.

Miami's current organizer diagram is undated; 2026 named grandstands were
cross-checked against the organizer's 2026 ticket announcement. Turn 1 East
is excluded. Montreal's current public site is for 2027; 2026 visitor/ticket
references and the OSM extract are recorded separately. Austin's position
diagram is from 2025, with its seven 2026 grandstand families confirmed by COTA.
Interlagos temporary sector extents are explicit rough GIS picks matched to an
isometric organizer diagram, not survey measurements. Las Vegas uses 2026
organizer grandstand diagrams, including the relocated Turn 3 stand.

All stand heights, slopes, rows, seating-band cuts and fences are estimates.
Missing ticket sectors and general-admission boundaries are not fabricated.
