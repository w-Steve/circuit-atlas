# Historical circuit atlas

Source: F1DB contributors, F1DB v2026.17.0 (2026-10-11).
https://github.com/f1db/f1db/releases/tag/v2026.17.0
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adaptations: selected circuit coordinates/names, championship race dates and layout
identifiers; grouped by venue; Chinese display names and aliases added. Race results,
driver data and standings are not included. The separate 2026 scheduled calendar is
reconciled to the project's Formula1.com snapshot checked 2026-10-23.

F1DB attribution is visible in the homepage and individual venue pages. Full license
is distributed alongside this notice as F1DB-CC-BY-4.0.txt. Raw four JSON source
extracts and hashes are retained in artifacts/atlas-source and src/atlas/provenance.json.
Downloaded release zip SHA256 (matches publisher checksum):
0f1717eaebae646b7ced9ca96f6be3282c1c1d435d0a9d75f2c00f40feb7d2a8

Coverage: 78 hosting venues, 1950–2026, held races through 2026-10-11; includes
1950–1960 Indianapolis 500 championship rounds. A venue is not a historical layout
reconstruction. Future calendar dates are not counted as held races.
