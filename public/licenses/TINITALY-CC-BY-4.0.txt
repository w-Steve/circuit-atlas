Changes: native 10m-aligned WCS Float32 window, 163x267 pixels in EPSG:32632.
Native numeric values are retained for reproducibility. Bilinear pixel-centre
resampling to a south-to-north 257x257 geographic grid, rounded to millimetres;
one horizontal direction is oversampled, which does not improve native 10m
resolution. A local reference altitude is subtracted for rendering. No vertical
scaling, image-derived heights, synthetic gap filling or visibility-fitted slope
adjustments. Runtime road/stand display cuts follow the shared scene system.

Regional compilation, released 2023 from older regional sources, not a 2026
circuit engineering survey or exact spectator-platform/fence-base elevation.
Source imagery, institutional logos and original maps are not distributed.
