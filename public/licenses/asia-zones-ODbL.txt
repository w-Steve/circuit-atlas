# Asia and Oceania spectator footprint data

© OpenStreetMap contributors. Raw footprint extracts in src/circuits/asia/osm
and OSM-derived boundaries in src/circuits/generated are available under ODbL:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Attribution: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Original OSM way IDs, tags and source dates are retained.

Temporary grandstand footprints are approximate factual interpretations of
publicly available venue diagrams. Sources, diagram years, control pairs and
model assumptions are retained in src/circuits/asia/review.json and
scripts/build-asia-spectators.py. Diagram-derived coordinates and estimated
viewing regions are not official ticket boundaries or surveyed seat positions.
Source map artwork, venue photographs, logos and ticket assets are not included.

Suzuka's organizer-authored 2026 wayfinding map was consulted through a retained
document copy at https://www.coloma.it/images/F1E-Japan-2026-Circuit-Map.pdf .
Melbourne's 2026 diagram is an independent annotated seating guide; it is not
misrepresented as an organizer engineering plan.
Baku position references are from the 2025 organizer wayfinding map, with 2026
name checks recorded separately. Singapore's formerly 2026 URL now serves a
2027 diagram: it is used only as a low-confidence positional reference for
corroborated 2026 named stands, never as evidence of 2026 ticket availability.

All stand heights, slopes, rows, seating-band cuts and fences are estimates.
No circuit or Formula One brand affiliation or seat-view guarantee is implied.
