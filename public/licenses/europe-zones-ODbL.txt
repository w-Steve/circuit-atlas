Original OSM way IDs, sources and retrieval dates are retained per zone.

Manually interpreted temporary viewing footprints are factual, approximate
placements from 2026 organizer diagrams and public venue descriptions. Source
URLs, ground controls, rectangle parameters and estimated registration offsets
are retained in review.json and scripts/build-europe-spectators.py. Heights,
slopes and fences are estimates, not OSM elevation surveys or seat guarantees.
No organizer map artwork, photographs, logos or ticket assets are distributed.

Hungaroring ordinary-bank row counts are factual numbers interpreted from
independent Oversteer48 seating charts and descriptions (checked 2026-10-24).
URLs and source confidence are retained in europe/hungaroring-structural-datums.json.
No third-party seating artwork or photographs are bundled. Estimated stair
risers and OSM building footprints are not a surveyed seat inventory.

The Hungaroring ordinary main seating strip is a low-confidence factual
interpretation of the existing OSM building footprint and the designer DVM
Group completed-project photographs: https://www.dvmgroup.com/en/references/projects/hungaroring .
Assumed seating inset, tread depth and uncertainty ranges are recorded with
the original building outline. Photographs, artwork and logos are not included.

Zandvoort GA1/GA2 factual viewing-area boundaries are interpreted from the official
2026 Dutch Grand Prix venue map (source URL and SHA256 retained in
src/circuits/europe/zandvoort-general-admission.json). Registration is approximate;
known road, stand, building and OSM-derived water footprints are conservatively
excluded. Neither the PDF artwork nor event logos/images are bundled. Terrain
uses the separately attributed GEDTM30 CC BY 4.0 data at true vertical scale.

Spa Gold 9 (reviewed 2026-10-24) is registered from the organizer SVG at
https://www.spagrandprix.com/en/ticketing/access-tickets using the retained
OSM Gold 1 rectangle as its approximate control. The SVG is undated; it is not
claimed as a precise 2026 survey. Only factual points/registration metadata are
retained in europe/spa-structural-datums.json. The original SVG/page artwork and
organizer panoramas are local review evidence and are not distributed.

Hungaroring Grid 2/3 name association was corrected on 2026-10-25 from the
2025 event wayfinding diagram retained at
https://gpdestinations.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025_F1-Hungary-Wayfinding-Map_Positive_VISUAL.pdf
and visitor location descriptions at
https://oversteer48.com/hungaroring-grid-3-grandstand/ .
Both original OSM polygons are retained unchanged; stale name tags are recorded

Monza P/Circolare Prato geographic portions use the organizer2026 diagram at
https://www.monzanet.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GP_F1_2026_LISTINO_PREZZI_6_17.pdf .
The six broad green strokes are approximate registered factual boundaries of
one ordinary admission family, not separate products or surveyed parcels.
Fifty public code/name identities (including separate GF and GT products) are
recorded independently from incomplete model coverage. No prices, PDF artwork,
logos or official typography are distributed. Controls, source hash, factual
coordinates and interpretation are retained in europe/monza-general-admission.json
and season-review/monza-public-inventory.json. Track/terrain retain their own
MIT/ODbL/CC BY4.0 notices; local barriers and terrain are not a seat-view guarantee.

Monza sourced chain-link polylines and explicit heights derive from OpenStreetMap
contributors under ODbL 1.0 (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
Seven way IDs, node coordinates, source versions/timestamps and response hashes
are retained in europe/monza-barriers.json. XML dumps and editor personal metadata
are not distributed. Procedural wire cells and post dimensions are schematic.
The Lombardy DTM5 candidate has not been downloaded or redistributed; its
metadata license does not imply that its heights are used by this application.

Monza code12 naming continuity uses factual organizer name/code information from
https://www.monzanet.it/en/circuit/ and
https://www.monzanet.it/en/amex-tickets-draft/ alongside the retained2026 price
tables. Source hashes and previous OSM footprint facts are retained in

copy is distributed. Product identity does not certify current seating geometry.
